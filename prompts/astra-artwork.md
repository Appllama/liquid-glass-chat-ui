# Cookbook 2 (Astra): artwork

The original asset notes describe the following generated artwork. The prompts below are replacement recipes reconstructed from those descriptions, not exact generation transcripts.

## Portraits

```text
Make a three-by-three contact sheet of nine different fictional adult portraits. Varied ages, faces, hair and clothing, candid natural expressions, warm neutral backgrounds and soft daylight. Head and shoulders, centered with room around each head for a circular crop. Nine equal square cells; no gutters, labels, text, logos, or watermark. Photographic skin texture and natural colors.
```

Export each square as `portraits/0.png` through `portraits/8.png`. The runtime uses the coastal photograph for the group portrait; index 4 is retained in the documented portrait set.

## Coastal photograph

```text
A quiet Northern California cove at golden hour. Muted turquoise water, olive headlands, pale rocks, and soft sea foam. Natural coastal haze and warm late light. A wide, calm photographic composition without people, buildings, text, logos, or watermark.
```

Save as `photos/coast.png`. The same local image appears in the group avatar, message attachment, and full-screen photo route.

## App icon

The included icon is a procedural three-sphere mark from the original implementation. Replace `assets/images/icon.png` with a 1024 x 1024 opaque PNG when adapting this cookbook to another identity.
